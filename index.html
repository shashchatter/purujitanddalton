Attire

What to Wear?

Our priority is that everyone feels good in what they wear. Wear what you will be comfortable and confident in. That means dance-ready outfits and, on Saturday (Mar. 13), an outfit that will last you from the afternoon baraat (festive procession) through the reception and beyond.

If you want clear guidelines, we’d suggest colorful cocktail attire for the Sangeet (Day 1) and formal attire for the ceremony and reception (Day 2). And remember, this is a GAY wedding: don’t be afraid to be too gorgeous or too glamorous (upstage the bride? what bride!?)!!

Attire

What about Indian attire?

Indian, South Asian, or Western attire is welcome for both days of the wedding. If you want to try Indian clothes, but only on one of the days, we’d recommend picking Friday, as the sangeet will be the most colorful event, and nowhere does brilliant colors and vibrant fabrics better than India!

Two easy airports

03 How we met

Two freshmen and one very long conversation.

Proof that even where fun goes to die, love finds a loophole.

Purujit and Dalton met in the winter/spring of 2016 — freshmen at the University of Chicago who were, in theory, there to study. Purujit had come from Coppell, just outside Dallas; Dalton from Tulsa. Their friends pushed them into a room together to see what happened. They started talking and, more or less, never stopped.

The years after weren't always easy — about five years long-distance through medical school and law school, with homes and frequent visits in Oakland, Houston, and New Orleans — before they moved back in together in Dallas. We'd love for you to be there as they make it official.
